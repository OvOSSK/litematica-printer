Litematica 0.15.4, MaLiLib 0.16.3. Separate flat world: Neko Hana Port Test.

Hana printer 0.7.2.1-hana-neko.1.

  could be placed back in the hotbar. Warehouse kept two material types.
- Four-stone schematic loaded and material list opened without an injection crash.
- With empty material inventory, warehouse material counting changed stone
  availability from 0 to 64, missing materials from 4 to 0.

  One player-obstructed placement completed after moving away.

  This is intentional upstream main-hand protection, not a failed return.

  printed four stone blocks. Warehouse total became 120 (64 planks + 56 stone).
- Survival manual round trip: left-click withdrew one stone (warehouse 55),
  picked it up from hotbar, ordinary left-click on occupied plank display
  deposited it; warehouse total returned to 120, no carried/extra stone remained.
- Printer work switch left OFF after validation.

Earlier checks in this test world: matching search, no-match search and clearing
search; direct deposit into empty and occupied display slots; stock survived restart.

Limits: this is a small single-player integration test, not exhaustive coverage
